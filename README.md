# Hello

If you've found your way here, welcome.

Hi I'm Jeremy.

I don't have everything figured out yet, and I don't think that's the point.

This repository isn't a collection of perfect projects or years of experience. It's a record of curiosity—of ideas that may or may not work, sketches that might become products, and questions I'm still trying to answer.

I'm fascinated by the intersection of technology, design, business, and culture. I want to understand why some products become part of people's lives while others are forgotten. More than anything, I want to spend my life building things that matter.

For now, I'm learning.

Learning how hardware is built.

Learning how great products are designed.

Learning how people think.

Learning how to turn ideas into reality.

If you're here for polished answers, you probably won't find them.

But if you're interested in following the journey of someone trying to make something wonderful, then you're looking at it.

Thanks for stopping by.

— Jeremy
